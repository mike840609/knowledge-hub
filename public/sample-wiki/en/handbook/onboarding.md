# Onboarding

Welcome aboard. This page lists what a new teammate does in the first days.

## First week

- Meet your manager and agree on what you will work on.
- Set up your laptop and accounts.
- Read the [leave policy](./leave-policy.md) so you know how time off works.

Planning a day off already? See [how to request leave](./leave-policy.md#how-to-request-leave).

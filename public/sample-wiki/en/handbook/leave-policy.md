# Leave policy

Everyone gets paid annual leave. This page explains how to take it.

## How to request leave

Tell your manager the dates at least a week ahead, then record them in the team calendar. Short notice is fine for sick leave; just let your manager know the same day.

## Approvals

Your manager approves each request, usually within two working days. Longer absences may also need a second approval.

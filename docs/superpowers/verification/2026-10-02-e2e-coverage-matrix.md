Rename, move, create, and archive refusals belong to different routes and UIs and cannot be combined merely because “all return 404.” `refuses an archived folder, and a document's node` in `organize-api.test.ts` protects rename; `refuses a destination that is archived, or is not a folder` protects move; new-document form refusal must be paired with the create case.

Next-stage priority candidates are pure organize-fixture creation: nonempty-folder refusal, non-tested siblings in archive/Undo cases, and the initial vanishing-folder fixture. First measure setup/assertion segments; retain New document here, archive, Undo, toast, and access-check assertions. Most Composer `createNote` calls already use APIs and cannot be counted again as new speedup gains. Other composer keyboard/render-timing cases are not currently candidates for deletion or moving layers.
